Who We Are

A builder, a developer, and your single point of contact.

Orchid Custom Homes was founded in Edmonton in 2019, born out of one persistent frustration: building a custom home meant juggling a disconnected cast of contractors, designers, and trades — each pointing at the other when something went sideways. We started Orchid to fix that. One accountable team, one contract, one phone number, from the day you buy your lot to the day we hand you the keys.

Over the past 7+ years we've delivered 15+ homes across Greater Edmonton — new infill on mature inner-city lots, family homes in growing communities, and investment builds for clients who want the numbers to make sense. We handle lot purchase, architectural design, City of Edmonton permitting, and full construction in-house, so nothing falls through the gaps between trades.

Whether you're a family planting roots in St. Albert or an investor developing a skinny infill in the core, we bring deep, street-by-street Edmonton knowledge — zoning, mature-neighbourhood overlays, soil, and the realities of building through an Alberta winter. The result is a home that's genuinely yours, built by people who answer the phone.
