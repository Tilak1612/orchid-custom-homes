Custom Home vs. Pre-Owned in Edmonton: The Real Math

Building new and buying resale rarely compare cleanly. Once you factor in lot cost, financing, possession timelines, energy efficiency and the renovations an older home will eventually need, the gap is usually smaller than buyers expect — and the new-build often wins on long-term value.

In this guide we walk through a realistic Edmonton scenario: a mature-neighbourhood lot, a single-family custom build, and a comparable resale home of similar size. We break down where the dollars actually go, how draw-schedule financing differs from a standard mortgage, and which trade-offs matter most for the way you plan to live.
