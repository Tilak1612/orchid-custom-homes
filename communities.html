Lot Strategy

Why location matters more than you think.

Zoning & infill potential

Edmonton's zoning bylaw determines whether a lot supports detached, duplex or multi-unit infill. We confirm what you can actually build before you commit.

Grade, soil & servicing

Lot grade, soil bearing and access to water, sewer and power directly affect your foundation and budget. We assess servicing on every site.

Resale & rental value

Established neighbourhoods like Glenora hold value differently than new SW communities. We help match the lot to your resale or rental goals.

Lifestyle & commute

Schools, river valley trails, LRT and commute times into central Edmonton shape daily life. The right area balances value with how you live.
