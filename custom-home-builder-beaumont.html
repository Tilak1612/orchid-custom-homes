Building in Beaumont

Your Beaumont home, built end to end.

Beaumont has grown quickly while keeping its distinct character — the landmark church on the hill and a strong community feel — alongside newer family communities like Dansereau Meadows, Four Seasons and Montrose. For buyers who want a newer home close to south Edmonton without building in the city itself, it's an excellent option.

Orchid carries the entire build for you — assessing the lot, producing the drawings, securing City of Beaumont approvals, coordinating engineering and trades, and managing construction through to handover. One team, one contract, one phone number.
