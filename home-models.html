Model 01 — Single Detached

The fully custom forever home.

Our most-requested build. Designed for families who want a home shaped entirely around how they live — open kitchens, flex spaces, mudrooms that survive an Edmonton winter, and primary suites that feel like a retreat.

Whether you're building on a new community lot in Keswick or a mature infill lot in Glenora, we tailor the floor plan, energy package and finishes to your site and budget. Triple-pane glazing, high-efficiency mechanicals and proper grading come standard — because they matter here.

Model 02 — Duplex

Two homes, one smart investment.

The investor and multi-generational favourite. A side-by-side duplex turns a single mature lot into two saleable or rentable homes — often the strongest return Edmonton's infill bylaws allow on a standard lot.

We handle demolition, permitting and the full build, designing each side for privacy, light and rental appeal. Live in one side and rent the other, sell both as half-duplexes, or hold the pair for income — the format flexes to your strategy.

2 Units 1,300–1,700 sq ft / side Strong Rental Yield From $850K (both sides)
Explore a duplex build
Model 03 — Townhome

Efficient multi-unit living.

Townhomes deliver more units per lot at an attainable price point — ideal for first-time buyers, downsizers, and investors building a small rental portfolio in a growing Edmonton neighbourhood.

Modern stacked or row configurations make the most of narrower infill lots while keeping each unit bright, private and low-maintenance. We optimize unit mix, parking and shared servicing so the project pencils out and sells.

2–3 Beds 1,200–1,600 sq ft Low Maintenance From $410K / unit
Plan a townhome project
Model 04 — Multi-Family Infill

Scale up with serious infill.

For investors ready to go bigger, multi-family infill turns a well-located mature lot into a 4–8 unit income property. Edmonton's recent zoning changes have opened real opportunity for builders who understand the rules — and we do.

We coordinate land use, engineering, parking, servicing and construction as one accountable team, with a mixed unit layout designed for durable rental demand. You get a single point of contact from feasibility through to lease-up readiness.

4–8 Units Mixed Layouts Income Property From $1.6M project
Discuss a multi-family build
Model 05 — Quick Possession

Finished, landscaped, move-in ready.

Not everyone has 9–12 months to build. Quick possession homes are completed Orchid builds — fully finished, landscaped and ready for keys — for buyers who need to move on a tighter timeline without sacrificing quality.

Each home carries the same craftsmanship, warranty and energy standards as our custom builds; you simply skip the design phase. Availability changes month to month, so reach out for what's ready right now.
