Who We Are

A builder, a developer, and a guide for your entire project.

Orchid Custom Homes Ltd is an Edmonton-based custom home builder and development partner. We were founded on a simple frustration our clients kept sharing — that building a home meant juggling a dozen disconnected contractors, missed timelines, and unclear costs.

So we built the opposite. Orchid handles lot acquisition, architectural planning, city permitting, engineering, demolition, trades, construction and landscaping as a single, accountable team. Whether you're a family designing a forever home or an investor evaluating an infill or duplex opportunity, you get one partner who knows Edmonton's neighbourhoods, zoning, and market — and who stays with you from the first conversation to the day you get the keys.
