Overview

Designed by the people who'll build it.

Architectural planning is where the most important — and most expensive — decisions about your home are made. Where the light enters, how rooms flow, how the structure stands, how efficiently it heats through an Edmonton winter: all of it is set in the drawings. Orchid plans your home as the team that will also build it, so design and construction are never in conflict.

That distinction matters. Plans drawn in isolation often look beautiful and cost a fortune to build, or hide details that surface as change orders months later. Because our planners work shoulder to shoulder with our trades and project managers, every drawing is checked against real construction cost and constructability before it's finalized — and tuned to your lot's grade, orientation and the City's bylaws and energy requirements.

From a first concept sketch to a permit-ready, construction-grade set, we plan homes across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc and Beaumont.

Frequently Asked

Architectural planning questions.

What Edmonton homeowners ask before starting the design of a custom home.

Can you design my home and also build it?
Yes — that's the core advantage of working with Orchid. We plan your home as the team that will construct it, so the drawings are tested against real construction cost and buildability from the start. You avoid the common gap where a designer hands off a beautiful but expensive set, and the builder then has to value-engineer it back. Design and build stay aligned the whole way through.
Do you handle the energy-code requirements for Edmonton?
We do. Alberta's energy code shapes everything from window placement to insulation and mechanical sizing, and Edmonton's climate makes it more than a checkbox. We model the home's envelope and layout against the code during planning, so the design that goes for permit is already compliant and genuinely efficient to heat — not retrofitted to pass at the last minute.
Will I see the design before it's finalized?
Always. Planning is a staged, collaborative process with your sign-off at each step — brief, concept and massing, developed plans, then the construction set. We often work in 3D so you can see and feel the home before drawings are locked, which is the cheapest and easiest time to make changes. By the time we produce the construction set, the design genuinely reflects what you want.
