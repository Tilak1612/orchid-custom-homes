Overview

More home, without leaving the one you have.

A basement is the most cost-effective square footage you'll ever add. Done right, it becomes a legal rental suite, an in-law space, a home theatre, a gym, or simply the family room you've always wanted — done wrong, it becomes a moisture, permit and resale headache. Orchid handles basements the same way we handle a full build: properly engineered, properly permitted, and properly finished.

Because design, permitting, engineering and trades all live under one roof, the person drawing your basement talks daily to the people permitting and building it. Egress windows, suite separations, plumbing rough-ins and HVAC are coordinated up front — not discovered halfway through. That continuity is what keeps a basement project on budget, code-compliant, and free of the surprises that stall most renovations.

We develop and renovate basements across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc and Beaumont — from straightforward finishing to legal secondary suites and full underpinning.

Frequently Asked

Basement development questions.

What Edmonton homeowners most often ask about finishing, suites and basement renovations.

Can you build a legal basement suite in Edmonton?
Yes. We design and permit secondary suites to City of Edmonton requirements — separate entrance, egress, fire separation, ventilation and parking — so the suite is fully legal to rent.
Do I need a permit to finish my basement?
Almost always. Finishing, adding bedrooms, plumbing or a suite triggers building and trade permits. We handle the permitting and inspections as part of the project.
How long does a basement development take?
Most finished basements run several weeks to a few months depending on size, plumbing, and whether it's a legal suite. We give you a fixed scope and timeline before we start.
My basement has low ceilings — can that be fixed?
Often, yes — through underpinning or bench footings to lower the floor. It's an engineered structural job, and it's one we do in-house on the right projects.
Can you fix moisture or water issues first?
Yes. We assess and address drainage, sealing and waterproofing before finishing, so you're not building over a future problem.
