Overview

We speak the City's language.

Most build delays don't happen on site — they happen at the permit counter. A submission that's missing a document, a plan that quietly breaches a setback, a development permit that triggers a neighbour notification: each can add weeks. Orchid manages the whole permitting process so those surprises are caught and resolved before they cost you time.

Because we permit Edmonton projects constantly, we know what the City's reviewers look for, how development and building permits sequence together, and where mature-neighbourhood and infill rules differ from new communities. We assemble complete, correct submissions the first time, track them through review, and respond to conditions and requests for information promptly — so your permit keeps moving instead of sitting in a queue.

We handle permitting for projects across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc and Beaumont, adapting to each municipality's process.

Frequently Asked

City permitting questions.

What Edmonton homeowners and investors ask about permits before they build.

What's the difference between a development and a building permit?
A development permit confirms that what you want to build is allowed on the lot — its use, height, size and placement under the zoning bylaw. A building permit then confirms how it will be built meets the building code, with construction drawings and engineering. Most Edmonton homes need both, and they sequence in a specific order. We manage both streams so they line up correctly and nothing waits on a missing approval.
Why do Edmonton permits sometimes take so long?
The biggest cause of delay isn't the City — it's incomplete or non-compliant submissions that bounce back for revision, each round adding weeks. Infill and mature-neighbourhood projects can also trigger additional reviews or neighbour notifications. We reduce delay by pre-checking compliance and assembling complete packages up front, then responding to any City requests immediately rather than letting the file sit.
Do I need to deal with the City myself?
No. We act as your point of contact for the permitting process, from preparing and submitting applications to fielding the City's questions and coordinating inspections. You stay informed at each milestone, but the legwork, paperwork and follow-up are ours. Because permitting connects directly to our design and construction teams, any required change flows straight through without a separate handoff.
