Overview

A home built around your life — not a catalogue.

Custom home building with Orchid begins with a conversation about how you live: where the light should fall, how the kitchen connects to the family, how the home should age with you. From there our architectural and construction teams translate that vision into a buildable, fixed-scope plan tuned to your lot, your budget and Edmonton's climate and energy code.

Because every discipline lives under one roof, the person designing your home talks daily to the people permitting, engineering and building it. Nothing gets lost between a designer and a framer, or a permit office and a trades crew. That continuity is what keeps a true custom build on budget and on schedule — and what lets us hold one accountable point of contact from first sketch to final walkthrough.

We build detached and estate homes across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc and Beaumont, applying the same craftsmanship and the same in-house model to every project regardless of size.

Frequently Asked

Custom home building questions.

What Edmonton families most often ask before starting a custom build with Orchid.

How long does a custom home take to build in Edmonton?
Timelines vary with size, site and selections, but most Edmonton custom homes run through a design and permitting phase followed by construction. Because we handle permits and engineering in-house, we plan around Edmonton's permit timelines and winter build conditions from the start, rather than discovering them mid-project. We give you a realistic schedule before you commit and update it at every milestone.
Do I need to own a lot before I start?
No. Many of our clients come to us before they own land. We help you source and evaluate lots across Edmonton and the surrounding communities — checking zoning, grade, servicing and resale potential — so the site actually suits the home you want. If you already own a lot, we assess it for the same factors before we design.
How is a custom home different from a production build?
A production home starts from a fixed set of plans and limited options. A custom home starts from you — your lot, your lifestyle and your priorities — and the plan is drawn to fit. With Orchid you also get a single accountable team for design, permits, engineering and construction, so the coordination that often falls on the homeowner in a fragmented build is handled for you.
