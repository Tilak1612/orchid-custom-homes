Overview

Every system, one schedule.

The wiring, gas lines, plumbing, heating and ventilation are the systems you never see and rely on every single day. Get the rough-in right and the home runs quietly for decades; get the sequencing wrong and you're opening finished walls to fix what should have been coordinated up front. Orchid manages all of it as one trade package on a single schedule.

Each of these systems is installed by licensed trades and inspected to the Alberta and City of Edmonton requirements that apply. What we add is coordination — making sure the HVAC ducting, the plumbing stacks and the electrical runs are all planned together before rough-in, so they don't fight for the same space inside your walls and ceilings.

That in-house sequencing is exactly where multi-contractor builds tend to break down. When the electrician, the plumber and the HVAC crew answer to the same project manager, the inspections line up, the cover-up happens once, and your finishing trades aren't left waiting on a system that wasn't ready.

Frequently Asked

Trades questions, answered.

What Edmonton homeowners most often ask about the electrical, gas and mechanical work in their build.

Are these trades licensed, or are they handled in-house?
The electrical, gas, plumbing, HVAC and mechanical work is performed by licensed trades and inspected to the Alberta and City of Edmonton requirements that apply to your build. What Orchid manages in-house is the coordination, scheduling and inspection sequencing — so you have one project manager accountable for the systems rather than five separate contractors to chase.
Why does sequencing these systems matter so much?
Electrical runs, plumbing stacks and HVAC ducting all compete for space inside your walls, floors and ceilings. If they're not planned together, one trade ends up cutting into another's work — or a finished wall has to be reopened. By coordinating the rough-in and lining up inspections before drywall, we close the walls once and avoid the rework that drives up cost and timeline on poorly managed builds.
How do you size heating and ventilation for Edmonton winters?
Edmonton's climate means heating and ventilation are designed around real winter loads and the energy-efficiency requirements in the building code. We coordinate high-efficiency furnaces, heat-recovery ventilation and properly sized ducting so the home holds temperature evenly, manages humidity, and stays efficient through the coldest months rather than running flat-out to keep up.
