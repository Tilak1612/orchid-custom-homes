Overview

A builder's eye before you buy.

Most buyers evaluate a lot the way they'd evaluate a house — location, size, price. But a building lot has a second, hidden layer: how it's zoned, how it drains, where the services sit, what it will cost to prepare, and what the City will and won't approve on it. Orchid reads that layer for you before you commit.

Because we build on these lots every day, our assessment is grounded in real construction costs and real Edmonton bylaws — not guesswork. We'll tell you plainly whether a parcel suits the home you have in mind, what it would take to make it work, or whether you'd be better walking away. That candor up front is what prevents the most expensive surprises later, after the land is already yours.

We assist across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc and Beaumont — from mature-neighbourhood infill parcels to new-community and acreage lots.

What's Included

What we check before you commit.

Each lot we assess gets the same builder-grade review — these are the concrete things we evaluate so you buy land that can actually carry your home.

Zoning & bylaw fit

We confirm what the zone permits — height, setbacks, coverage and use — so your plan won't be refused.

Grade & drainage

We read slope, elevation and how water moves so grading and lot-prep costs don't blindside your budget.

Servicing & utilities

We check water, sewer, gas and power access and what hookups or upgrades the build will require.

Buildability report

A plain-language summary of what the lot can hold, what it will cost to prep, and any red flags.

Resale potential

An honest read on how the finished home will sit in the neighbourhood and hold value long term.

Offer guidance

Frequently Asked

Lot purchase questions.

What buyers ask before choosing land for a custom home or infill in Edmonton.

Are you a realtor — do you sell the lots?
No — we're your builder, not your agent. We work alongside your realtor (or can point you to one) and bring the construction perspective they typically can't: whether a lot can actually carry the home you want, what site prep will cost, and what the City will approve. You get an independent, builder-grade read before you make an offer.
What's the most common problem you catch on Edmonton lots?
Grade and servicing surprises are the big ones. A lot can look perfect and still need significant grading, fill or a costly utility connection that wasn't priced into the purchase. On mature infill lots, zoning limits and setback rules are the other frequent catch — they can quietly shrink the home you're allowed to build. We surface both before you commit.
Can you assess a lot I've already found myself?
Absolutely, and many clients do exactly that. Send us the listing or address and we'll review the zoning, grade, servicing and buildability, then give you a clear verdict on whether it suits your plans and what it would take to build there. If it checks out, we carry the same information straight into the design phase.
