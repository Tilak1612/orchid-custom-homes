Overview

One project manager, one accountable site.

Construction is where a home is won or lost. The plans can be perfect and the permits clean, but if the trades aren't sequenced, the schedule slips and the quality suffers. Orchid runs construction as a single, tightly managed operation — every trade booked, inspected and signed off by one project manager who owns the whole site.

That's the difference between a coordinated build and a collection of contractors. Because our electrical, gas, plumbing, HVAC, mechanical and finishing trades work under the same roof and the same schedule, the sequence stays tight and nothing gets re-opened later. You get one point of contact, regular milestone updates, and a build that moves predictably rather than lurching between subcontractors who've never worked together.

We build across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc and Beaumont — applying the same managed, transparent process whether it's a single custom home or a multi-unit infill.

Frequently Asked

Project construction questions.

What Edmonton clients ask about how Orchid runs the build itself.

Will I have one point of contact during construction?
Yes. Every Orchid build is run by one dedicated project manager who owns your site from foundation to final walkthrough. They schedule and inspect every trade, control quality and keep you updated, so you're never chasing separate subcontractors or piecing the story together yourself. One person is accountable for your home — and they're who you call with any question.
How do you keep the trades coordinated?
Our electrical, gas, plumbing, HVAC, mechanical and finishing trades work under the same roof and the same schedule, managed by your project manager. That means each trade arrives in the right sequence, is inspected before the next stage covers its work, and isn't re-opened later because something was missed. It's the opposite of stitching together independent subs who've never worked together and don't share a timeline.
How will I know how the build is progressing?
You get regular, transparent milestone updates at each construction stage — foundation, framing, systems, finishing — so you always know where things stand and what's next. There are no black-box stretches where you wonder what's happening on site. If something needs a decision from you, your project manager raises it early, with the context to make the call confidently.
