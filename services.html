How It Fits Together

Three phases, one continuous handoff.

Most builds stall at the seams — between the architect and the permit office, the demolition crew and the framer, the finishing trades and the lender. Orchid removes those seams by owning each phase and carrying the project cleanly into the next.

Phase 01

Plan & Acquire

Before a single shovel hits the ground, we help you get the foundations right — the lot, the drawings, the approvals and the engineering. We assess Edmonton lots for zoning, grade, servicing and resale, then translate your goals into construction-ready architectural plans tuned to local bylaws and energy code.

From there we shepherd your development and building permits through the City of Edmonton and coordinate the structural, geotechnical and grading engineering. Getting this phase right is what keeps Edmonton builds on schedule — most delays trace back to permitting and soil surprises, and we plan for both.

Phase 02

Prepare & Build

On infill sites we handle permitted demolition, abatement, utility disconnects and haul-away, then prepare and pour the foundation. From framing up, your build runs under a single project manager who schedules and inspects every trade — electrical, gas, plumbing, HVAC and mechanical — so the sequence stays tight and nothing gets re-opened later.

This is where the one-team model earns its keep: instead of chasing separate contractors, you get coordinated trades, regular milestone updates, and one person accountable for the whole site from teardown to topping out.

Phase 03

Finish & Hand Over

Interior finishing brings the home to life — cabinetry, millwork, flooring, tile and the final selections you made during design. Outside, our crews handle grading, hardscape, driveways, decks and planting so the property is complete the day you take possession, not months after.

We close with a detailed final walkthrough, warranty documentation, and the keys. Throughout the project we also guide funding — from construction-financing introductions to draw management — so your budget and lender stay aligned right to handover.

Good to Know

Service FAQs.

Common questions from Edmonton families and investors about how our full-service model works.

Do you only build, or can you help me buy a lot?
Both. Many clients come to us before they own land. We help you source and evaluate lots across Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc and Beaumont — checking zoning, grade, servicing and resale potential — so you don't buy a site that limits the home you want or surprises you with costs once construction starts.
Can Orchid handle the demolition and permits for an infill?
Yes — infill is a core part of what we do. We manage permitted demolition, abatement, utility disconnects and haul-away, and we navigate development and building permits with the City of Edmonton on your behalf. Because the same team carries the project from teardown through engineering and construction, your infill stays on one timeline with one point of contact.
What areas do you serve?
We build across Greater Edmonton — including Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc and Beaumont. From mature-neighbourhood infill in central Edmonton to detached and estate builds in the surrounding communities, we know the local zoning, soil conditions and permitting processes in each.
Do you build duplexes and multi-family for investors?
Yes. We design and deliver single, duplex and multi-family infill formats wherever Edmonton zoning allows, and we work regularly with investors who care about timeline, budget and yield. We'll assess a mature lot's development potential up front and manage the full build — demolition, permits, engineering and construction — so the numbers stay predictable.
Can you help with construction financing?
We can point you in the right direction. Orchid provides construction-financing introductions and helps you understand how draws work, then manages the draw schedule against build milestones so your lender and budget stay aligned. We're not a lender ourselves — the financing terms come from your chosen institution — but we make the process clearer and keep it on track.
